How to use this pack (about 4–5 hours of work)
  1. Read a section once, slowly. Every section follows the same order: the idea in plain words, the recipe, a fully worked example, then the traps.
  2. Close the PDF and write the model answer or the function from memory on paper. Then compare. This is the step that matters. Reading alone is how you ended up with 27.
  3. Do the Grill section (section 7) without looking. The answers are on separate pages after it.
  4. Anything you got wrong: redo it tomorrow morning before the exam.
