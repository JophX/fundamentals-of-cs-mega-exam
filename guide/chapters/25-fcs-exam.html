25.1 Practice exam (new questions)

  1. (6 p) True/false: (a) Every regular language is context-free. (b) The complement of a CFL is CF. (c) If \(L\) is RE and \(\overline L\) is RE then \(L\) is recursive. (d) \(\{a^nb^n\}\cap a^*b^*\) is regular. (e) NFAs accept more languages than DFAs. (f) If A ≤ₚ B and B ∈ P then A ∈ P.
  2. (6 p) Give a DFA for strings over {a,b} that contain ab and have even length.
  3. (6 p) Convert the NFA \(p_0\xrightarrow{a,b}p_0,\ p_0\xrightarrow{b}p_1,\ p_1\xrightarrow{a}p_2\) (final \(p_2\)) to a DFA.
  4. (8 p) Prove \(\{a^nb^m\mid n>m\}\) is not regular.
  5. (8 p) Convert \(S\to aSb\mid SS\mid\lambda\) to CNF and run CYK on aabb.
  6. (8 p) Prove \(\{a^nb^nc^n\}\) is not context-free.
  7. (6 p) Show that the problem “does TM M ever write the symbol 1 on input w?” is undecidable.
Solutions

1. (a) T (b) F (c) T (d) F — it equals \(\{a^nb^n\}\) (e) F (f) T.

2. Product of “seen ab” (3 states: none / last was a / seen) and parity (2 states): 6 states, final = (seen, even).

3. Language: ends in ba. DFA states \(\{p_0\},\{p_0,p_1\},\{p_0,p_2\}\): \(\{p_0\}\xrightarrow{a}\{p_0\}\), \(\{p_0\}\xrightarrow{b}\{p_0,p_1\}\), \(\{p_0,p_1\}\xrightarrow{a}\{p_0,p_2\}\), \(\{p_0,p_1\}\xrightarrow{b}\{p_0,p_1\}\), \(\{p_0,p_2\}\xrightarrow{a}\{p_0\}\), \(\{p_0,p_2\}\xrightarrow{b}\{p_0,p_1\}\); final \(\{p_0,p_2\}\).

4. \(w=a^{n+1}b^n\); \(y=a^j\); \(i=0\) gives \(a^{n+1-j}b^n\) with \(n+1-j\le n\): not in L.

5. Nullable S. Remove λ: \(S\to aSb\mid ab\mid SS\) (and \(S\to S\), dropped). CNF: \(S\to AX\mid AB\mid SS,\ X\to SB,\ A\to a,\ B\to b\). CYK on aabb: row1 A,A,B,B; row2 ∅,S,∅; row3 ∅ (a|abb: A·∅; aa|bb: ∅), X (abb = ab|b: S·B → X); row4: a|abb: A·X → S. S ∈ top ⇒ accepted.

6. Ch. 11 Example 1.

7. Reduce halting: given M, w, build M′ that simulates M using a tape alphabet without 1 (rename 1 to a new symbol), and when M would halt, writes 1. M′ writes 1 on w ⇔ M halts on w.
